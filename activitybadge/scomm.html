Communicator Activity Badge

Communication can help us reach out to more people, from longer distances and using a wide range of languages.

Choose one of the following options:

  • Already hold or are working towards:
    • Radio Amateur Licence (Foundation, Intermediate or Full)
  • Otherwise, complete all of these:
    • Learn the regulations governing the use of amateur radio equipment.
    • Visit an amateur radio station.
    • Log 25 different amateur radio stations.
    • Show how to tune a simple communications receiver.
    • Give a typical greetings message.
    • Explain how radio waves travel globally.
    • Learn common HF/VHF amateur bands.
    • Learn the phonetic alphabet & at least 8 Q codes.
    • Recognize UK and nearby call signs.

  • Send/receive a short message by Morse or Semaphore (5 wpm).
  • Follow proper message sending/receiving procedures.
  • Learn the International Phonetic Alphabet & 8 Q codes.
  • Build a simple Morse code oscillator & send a message.

Tips for Leaders

Get involved in Jamboree on the Air (JOTA) or Internet (JOTI) to help Scouts earn this badge. Email joti@scouts.org.uk for more info.

Requirements can be adapted to suit each young person's abilities.
